# Elsewhere: Time Zone Overlap

Elsewhere lives in your menu bar (Mac) or system tray (Windows) and shows what time it is in the cities that matter to you, and when everyone's working hours overlap.

- One row per city with a 24-hour strip: night, evening and working hours are colour coded.
- Click or drag to pick an hour and see the local time in every city. Drag the edges to widen it into a meeting window.
- Star cities to show their times right in the menu bar (Mac), with the weekday when it is another day there.
- Set your own working hours, mark a home city, sort and arrange the rows, and choose a 12-hour or 24-hour clock.
- Light and dark appearance, zoom, a global shortcut and launch at login.

## Privacy policy

Elsewhere does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- Your list of cities and your settings are kept only on your own computer.
- The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it has no server of its own.
- When you search for a city, the text you type is sent to a location lookup service to find the place. On the Mac this is Apple's location service, through macOS. On Windows it is the Open-Meteo geocoding service. Elsewhere itself never receives that text.

If this policy changes, this page will be updated. Contact: mlurker+elsewhere@gmail.com
